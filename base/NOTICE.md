`base.HEIC` and `base.mov` are derived from goLive's sample camera Live Photo (https://github.com/code-path/goLive), Apache License 2.0 (see LICENSE). They keep only what iOS checks before accepting a generated clip as a moving lock screen wallpaper: `base.mov` holds the original's two timed metadata tracks (IDs 2 and 3: live-photo-info and still-image-time) and its tags, without its video; `base.HEIC` is a 16x16 placeholder carrying the original photo's tags. Wallpapers built from these are byte-identical in tracks and tags to ones built from the full originals.
